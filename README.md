# BC Clock Check

A tiny Android app concept that helps people in British Columbia understand whether they need to change their clocks after B.C.'s 2026 time-change update.

## Features

- Big plain-English answer: “Do I need to change my clocks?”
- Region selector for different B.C. areas
- Time comparison cards for Seattle, Calgary, Toronto, and London
- Widget preview concept
- No accounts
- No analytics
- No personal data collection

## Privacy

BC Clock Check does not collect, store, sell, or share personal information.
